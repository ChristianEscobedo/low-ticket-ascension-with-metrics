The Sh*t Test: 

Two things will happen when you do this. 

A. They agree and set a call. If that is the case then proceed with step 14. 

B. they reject the call and instead ask for you to send the free resource first. This is a sh*t test and this is their way of saying “I’m not interested enough, send me your thing and I’ll see”. 

To combat this, we need to take back the frame. Here’s how. 

Start by trying to understand why they don’t want to get on a call, and set expectations of what will be discussed. 

“Okay. Is there any particular reason why you don't want to have a call? Is there another way I can check in?” 

Then based on their reply, position the call as extra needed support that they NEED for the content/video you send them to actually work. 

“I understand you are used to people selling right away, but I genuinely just want this to work for you. If not by a call, then that’s all good.” 

[Send] 

“But how else can I ensure you understand and action this out?”

They will now have 2 responses, either A. They will let down their defense and book a call. B. They will convince you they will actually take action. Leverage this by doing the following. 

Best case: They commit to a call 
Worst Case: They reject the call but give you permission to hold them accountable.
